Chandabaj – Sound Test

Use headphones if you can. Every sound here is the same one the game uses. Move the sliders until it feels right, then tell me the numbers (button at the bottom).

Back to the game

1 · Volumes

Each slider makes a whole kind of sound louder or quieter.

2 · Vehicles that drive past

The engine sound belongs to the vehicle: it gets louder as it comes closer, shifts in pitch when it passes, and is heard from the left or the right. The road is drawn below – you are the yellow dot.

3 · Horns, bells and bangs

4 · The bazaar

Crowd noise, traffic hum, chatter from the people around you, and shop sounds chosen by the name on the shop sign.

5 · Voices and funny lines

The grey code in front of each line (like mob-3) is the file name to use if you record that line yourself: save it as audio/voice/mob-3.mp3 and add 'mob-3' to the list RECORDED at the bottom of js/fx/lines.js.

6 · Tell me how it sounds

Press the button, then copy the text and paste it in our chat together with what you want changed (for example “engines too loud, voices too quiet”).
